FAQ

Questions clients ask us first.

How fast can you send candidates?

Within 48 hours of understanding the role, we send a shortlist of 2-3 pre-screened candidates. The shortlist is free and you decide whether to interview.

Where are your developers based?

Our teams are in Romania and Poland, inside the EU. Poland shares the time zone of most of Western Europe, and Romania is one hour ahead, so working hours overlap fully.

Which technologies and languages do you cover?

The full range of modern technologies, from Java, .NET, Python and JavaScript to mobile, data, AWS, Azure and DevOps. Our team covers all 24 official EU languages.

How do you price your services?

Our rates are competitive because we are a small team with low overhead. We quote per role or per project after a short call, so you only pay for what you actually need.

Is there any commitment for the first step?

No. The 15-minute call, the CV shortlist and the project estimate are all free, with no contract and no obligation.

Which contracts and invoicing do you work with?

You contract with an EU company, UNITECH DIGITAL sp. z o.o. in Poland or UNITECH DIGITAL SOLUTIONS S.R.L. in Romania, with standard EU invoicing.
